Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026.

Helpen is a private journal. You write down one good thing you did each day, and the app grows a garden from it. There is no feed, no friends, and nothing you write is shown to anyone else.

What we collect

Your email address. Used only to sign you in. We send a six-digit code to it. We do not send marketing email.

Your name, as you type it. Shown in the greeting on your own screen.

Your entries. The text you write, the date each entry belongs to, and when it was created and last changed.

That is everything. We do not collect your location, your contacts, your photos, your device identifiers, or any advertising identifier. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party tracker in this app.

How it is stored

Entries are written to your phone first, and the app works fully offline. When you have a connection they are copied to our database so they survive losing your phone and follow you to a new one. That database is hosted by Supabase (supabase.com), and row-level security means a signed-in account can read and write only its own rows.

Signing out erases the entries from your phone. They remain in your account until you delete it.

Who we share it with

Nobody. We do not sell your data, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not use it to train anything. Supabase hosts the database on our behalf and processes nothing on its own account.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account removes your account, your name, and every entry, from your phone and from our servers. It is immediate and cannot be undone.

You can also write to hello@helpen.app and ask us to do it.

Children

Helpen is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
