Helpen

A daily record of small kindnesses.

Write down one good thing you did each day. However small — a message sent, a seat given up, a favour done.

Every day you write, a flower grows. After a month you have a garden of what you have done.

What it isn't

There is no feed, no friends, no sharing, and no way for anyone else to read a word of it. Nothing is public. Nothing is scored. The garden is the only reward, and it is only ever yours.

It works without a signal

Everything you write is saved on your phone first, so writing, the log and the garden all work with no connection at all. When a connection returns, your journal is copied to your account so it survives a lost phone and follows you to a new one.
